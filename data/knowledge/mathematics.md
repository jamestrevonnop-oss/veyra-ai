# Mathematics

Mathematics is the study of structures, quantities, patterns, relationships, and logical reasoning.

Algebra uses symbols and equations to represent unknown quantities and relationships.

A percentage represents a proportion out of one hundred.

A triangle is a polygon with three sides. The interior angles of a triangle add up to 180 degrees in Euclidean geometry.

The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length by its width.

The Pythagorean theorem states that for a right triangle, a² + b² = c², where c is the length of the hypotenuse.
